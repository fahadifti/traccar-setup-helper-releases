Traccar Setup Helper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Traccar Setup Helper ("the app") helps you configure Traccar Client on your Android phone so it keeps tracking in the background, and can show your device's status from a Traccar server. It is operated by Fahad Iftikhar ("we"). Contact: fahad.iftikhar@gmail.com.

What the app does with data

  • Device identifier you enter. If you use the server-status card, you type your Traccar device identifier. It is stored on your phone in encrypted storage and sent over HTTPS to the status service at track.generix.solutions so it can return that device's online status, name and last position time. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Information read on your phone only. The app reads your phone's brand, model and Android version, whether Traccar Client is installed, and whether Traccar Client has the permissions and settings it needs. This stays on your phone and is not transmitted or shared.
  • Alarm. The daily alarm is scheduled and played locally using your phone's default alarm sound. Nothing is recorded.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data.
  • The app does not read or collect your location. (Traccar Client, a separate app, handles location under its own terms and your server operator's policy.)

Server-side handling

The status service at track.generix.solutions is run by the same publisher. Requests are processed to answer your query. Standard web-server logs may include your IP address and the identifier you queried, and are used only to operate and secure the service. To ask for deletion of data held about you, email the contact above.

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. The identifier is stored in Android's encrypted storage. The app contains no credentials that grant access to other users' data.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
